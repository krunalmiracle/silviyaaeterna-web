Silviya Aeterna shares practical notes on skincare, rest, family conversations and mental health. The aim is to ask useful questions, not promise a perfect routine or substitute for professional care.

Made for curious adults and families. Shared in Bulgarian and English, with space for your own perspective.

A living atlas of everyday life.

Four everyday themes. Choose the one that matters to you today.

An imagined garden. A real moment for reflection.

Skincare

Start with a gentle cleanser, moisturiser and daytime sun protection. Add one new product at a time, and notice how your skin responds instead of chasing a longer routine.

A question to explore

Which steps in your routine feel useful, and which are there out of habit? For persistent irritation or other skin concerns, ask a qualified clinician.

Wellbeing

Make room for sleep, breaks and time outside when you can. Notice what actually helps on a difficult day; a small repeatable habit matters more than a perfect plan.

A question to explore

What gave you a little breathing room today? Notice it without turning it into another task to complete.

Family & children

Small daily rituals can make family conversations easier: put a phone aside, ask one open question and listen before offering a fix. Different homes need different routines.

A question to explore

When was the last time you felt heard at home? What helped that conversation feel different?

Mental health

When feelings are hard to name, start with a simple check-in: what happened, how did it feel and who could listen? This space is for conversation, not diagnosis or therapy.

A question to explore

Who could you turn to when things feel difficult? You do not need to wait for a crisis to seek qualified support.
