Explore the themes

Follow your curiosity.

Six themes, connected by one idea: paying a little more attention. Choose a theme to find a question worth sitting with.

Astrology Numerology Skincare Wellbeing Family & children Mental health

Look up. Look within.

Astrology

Birth charts, transits and seasonal cycles as a symbolic language for reflection. An invitation to notice patterns, not a prediction to live by.

A question to explore

Which parts of a description feel familiar, and which do not? Leave room for both instead of letting a label define you.

Numerology

Life paths, repeating numbers and the meanings we give them. A lens for personal interpretation, not scientific evidence or a fixed destiny.

A question to explore

Is there a number you associate with a memory? What matters more to you: the number itself, or the story attached to it?

Make room for care.

Skincare

Simple routines, ingredient literacy and realistic expectations. Space to understand your skin without miracle promises or pressure to buy more.

A question to explore

Which steps in your routine feel useful, and which are there out of habit? For persistent irritation or other skin concerns, ask a qualified clinician.

Wellbeing

Attention, rest, habits and self-improvement, approached through psychology-informed education. Small observations rather than another standard to live up to.

A question to explore

What gave you a little breathing room today? Notice it without turning it into another task to complete.

Grow, in good company.

Family & children

Everyday routines, conversations and boundaries. Reflections for adults caring for children, with room for different families and imperfect days.

A question to explore

When was the last time you felt heard at home? What helped that conversation feel different?

Mental health

Clear language for feelings, support and asking for help. General education that respects the difference between a thoughtful conversation and professional care.

A question to explore

Who could you turn to when things feel difficult? You do not need to wait for a crisis to seek qualified support.

Curiosity, with both feet on the ground.

Silviya Aeterna is a personal project bringing together symbolic traditions, everyday care and psychology-informed reflection. Different ways of looking at life, without pretending they all offer the same kind of knowledge.

Made for curious adults and families. Shared in Bulgarian and English, with space for your own perspective.
