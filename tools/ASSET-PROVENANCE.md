# Current daylight imagery provenance

Six original raster PNG images were generated through the locally available OpenAI Codex OAuth image provider, one call per scene. The scenes are a silver-framed daylight conservatory, unbranded skincare still life, notebook pause, family table, two-chair conversation room and magnolia closing still life. Visual inspection found no identifiable logos or claims. These are illustrative images; they do not depict Silviya, a real location, her products, a therapeutic setting or an Oriflame endorsement.

`tools/ASSET-MANIFEST.json` records for each scene the generation prompt, provider response's requested model and reported size/quality where available, request ID where available, original PNG SHA-256, exact original dimensions, English and Bulgarian alt text, derivative filenames, dimensions, byte lengths and SHA-256. A provider request is not proof of a disclosed internal engine version. The full originals and generation response manifest are archived at `/opt/data/home/silviyaaeterna-daylight-source/` outside the web-serving allowlist. The manifest's `source_path` points to that development archive and is never returned by the server.

Using Pillow 12.3, each original was converted at quality 86 to full-size and 780px-wide WebP with Lanczos resize. The optimized WebPs contain no embedded XMP; the immutable hashes and prompt records in the development-only manifest are their sidecar provenance. The 1200x630 social PNG was composed from the new hero, a pale legibility veil and locally self-hosted font artwork. It is not a second generated image.

The eight retired dark/gold scene derivatives, including astrology and numerology, were removed from `assets/images/`. Prior development-only preparation scripts, summaries and old `.impeccable` evidence are historical, not a reproduction procedure for the current artwork. The public site serves only the six current scenes and their responsive derivatives.
